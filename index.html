How to Play

Grow

1
Tap the hex you stand on to found your Headquarters. Your first hex is free.
2
Tap an outlined hex next to your land, within reach, to claim it. A 🌱 plant grows there.
3
Plants make credits. Tap a plant to collect. Hold a plant for 3 seconds to collect from every plant in reach.
4
Tap a plant with nothing to collect to open it: grow it (it evolves every 10 levels) and add boosters.
5
Buy buildings in the 🛒 Shop and place them on your plants.

Fight

1
Tap an animal, a player or a rival's hex, then tap Fire. Up close you hit more often.
2
Defeated animals drop boosters. Turn them on in your bag, or put plant boosters into a plant.
3
Knock-outs and wilted plants give you part of the owner's credits.

You can only act near where you really are. Anything further away needs a real walk.

Google Maps

The game uses Apple Maps when the server has an Apple Maps key. Until then it uses the free OpenStreetMap map. With your own Google Maps API key, this device uses Google Maps instead.

API Key

How to Get a Key

1
In Google Cloud Console, create a project and link a billing account.
2
Enable the Maps JavaScript API and create an API key.
3
Restrict the key to the website , paste it above and tap Save.
📍

Allow Location Access

1
In Safari, open the page menu next to the address (aA or ⋯), then tap Website Settings → Location → Allow.
2
In Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services, make sure Location Services is on and Safari Websites is set to While Using the App.
3
Come back here and tap Try Again.

Pages inside other apps, like the Claude app, can't use your location. Open the game's address in Safari, or use the iPhone app.
